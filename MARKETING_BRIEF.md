# MagnetEngine — Marketing Brief

> **For:** Marketers, copywriters, ad teams, and content creators  
> **Purpose:** Single source of truth for positioning, messaging, and campaign strategy

## 1. One-Sentence Description

MagnetEngine is an AI-powered Instagram lead generation and outreach platform that finds qualified prospects, writes a unique personalised DM for each one, lets you review and approve every message before it sends, then delivers them automatically — so agencies and consultants can fill their calendar without doing any manual prospecting.

## 2. The Problem — and Who Has It

### The old way is broken

Any agency owner, coach, consultant, or freelancer who relies on cold Instagram DMs to win clients has lived this cycle:

1. **Spend hours finding prospects manually** — scrolling hashtags, checking follower counts, opening profiles one by one, copying handles into a spreadsheet.
2. **Write the same tired opener** — "Hey, quick question…" or "Hi [NAME], I help coaches get clients." Sent to 50 people this week. Three opened it. Zero replied.
3. **Watch the calendar stay empty** — outreach is inconsistent because it's exhausting, so pipeline dries up between client work.
4. **Try a "bot"** — the tool blasts identical messages at scale, gets the account flagged, and destroys credibility overnight.

The underlying pain is a paradox: **personalisation at scale is impossible to do manually, and automation without personalisation doesn't work.**

### Who feels this pain most acutely

- **Digital marketing & growth agencies** trying to fill their own client pipeline while also running client campaigns
- **Online coaches and course creators** whose revenue depends on converting cold DMs into discovery calls
- **B2B service providers** (SMMA, SEO, paid ads, web design) who sell retainers starting at $1,000+/month
- **Freelancers scaling to an agency model** who need a consistent, repeatable system without hiring an SDR
- **Sales development reps** at agencies who currently spend 60% of their time on research and copywriting, not closing

The common denominator: they have a high-value offer, a clear ideal customer, and an Instagram audience full of those customers — but no scalable way to reach them with messages that get replies.

## 4. Core Workflow — Step by Step

This is what a user actually does from login to booked call. The whole loop takes under 20 minutes to set up and runs on near-autopilot after that.

### Step 1 — Configure: AI Prompt Wizard (one-time, ~5 minutes)

In **Settings**, a 4-step guided wizard asks the user:
- Their business name and niche
- Their ideal target audience description
- Their core value proposition (what outcome do they deliver?)
- An example of a DM that previously got a great reply (optional, used for style matching)
- Their preferred tone: *Casual / Friendly / Professional / Bold*

The wizard generates a detailed AI system prompt behind the scenes — the instructions the AI uses every time it writes a DM. No technical knowledge needed. Users can also paste in a winning DM from their own history and the AI will mirror its style.

### Step 2 — Set Filters: Lead Quality Rules (one-time, adjustable)

Still in **Settings**, the user sets automatic quality filters that are applied to every batch of leads after scraping:
- Minimum and maximum follower count (e.g. 5,000 – 500,000)
- Must-include keywords in the bio (e.g. "founder, CEO, agency, coach")
- Exclude keywords (e.g. "bot, giveaway, follow for follow")
- Account type: all, public only, or private only

These rules run automatically in the background — the user never has to manually filter again.

### Step 3 — Find Leads: Campaign Builder

In the **Campaign Builder**, the user runs an Instagram search:
- **What to search:** Enter comma-separated terms (e.g. "coaches, consultants, agency owner, skool")
- **Search type:** Profiles (find accounts matching keywords), Hashtags (explore a niche via hashtag), or Locations (find businesses in a specific city)
- **Volume:** Choose how many results to pull per search term (up to 250)
- **Optional enrichment:** Toggle on Facebook page + email enrichment for the top 10 results per term

Hit **Start Search**. MagnetEngine scrapes Instagram via a cloud service in the background (typically takes 1–4 minutes). Results appear with profile pictures, handles, follower counts, bios, and verification/business account tags. The user can review the list, uncheck any profiles they don't want, then click **Add to Queue**.

### Step 4 — Generate: AI Writes Every DM

In the **Approval Queue**, the user clicks **Generate AI DMs**. For every lead in the queue, the AI reads that specific profile — their bio, niche, follower count, whether they're a business account — and writes a unique, contextual message using the system prompt the wizard built. Each DM is:
- 2–3 sentences maximum
- References something specific from their profile
- Ends with a soft question or curiosity hook, not a hard pitch
- Sounds human-typed, not corporate

### Step 5 — Review: Human Approval (the key differentiator)

Every generated DM sits in the queue with a **Pending / Ready / Approved / Rejected** status. The user reviews each one:
- **Edit** any DM inline with a click
- **Approve** to mark it ready for sending
- **Reject** any lead or message they don't want sent
- **Delete** leads entirely from the pipeline

Only explicitly approved DMs ever get sent. The user is always in control.

### Step 6 — Send: Chrome Extension Delivers Messages

Clicking **Send Approved to Extension** pushes all approved leads and their DMs to a Chrome browser extension. The extension logs into the user's Instagram and sends each DM with randomised human-like delays (15–45 minutes between messages in production mode) to look and behave like a real person, not a bot.

- **Test Mode:** 30-second delays — useful for verifying setup
- **Production Mode:** 15–45 minute random delays — safe for real campaigns

### Step 7 — Track: Dashboard & AI Analyst

The **Dashboard** shows 8 live metrics across the full funnel:

| Metric | What it tracks |
|---|---|
| Total Leads | Profiles in the pipeline |
| DMs Sent | Outreach volume |
| Reply Rate | % of sent DMs that received any reply |
| Positive Reply Rate | % of replies showing genuine interest |
| Booking Rate | % of warm replies that converted to a booked call |
| Follow-up Rate | % of leads that received a follow-up message |
| Leads Contacted | Unique profiles reached |
| Active Campaigns | Number of distinct search campaigns run |

The **AI Analyst** reads these numbers and gives the user 2–3 prioritised, plain-English insights — e.g. "Your reply rate is below 5%. Try shortening your DMs to 1–2 sentences and lead with a question instead of a pitch." Benchmarks and recommendations update in real time.

### Step 8 — Manage: CRM & Export

The built-in CRM lets users update lead statuses (cold → warm → won), track the full conversation history, and export any filtered subset of their pipeline to CSV or JSON for use in external tools, follow-up sequences, or client reporting.

## 5. Key Features and Why Each Matters

### Instagram Profile Scraper (via Cloud)
**What:** Searches Instagram by keyword, hashtag, or location and pulls up to 250 profiles per search term. Runs in the cloud — no browser left open, no manual copying.  
**Why it matters:** Eliminates the most time-consuming part of cold outreach. A search that would take an SDR half a day to build manually takes 2–4 minutes here.

### Smart Lead Filtering Engine
**What:** Automatically removes profiles that don't match the user's ICP based on follower count, bio keywords, and account type — before any AI generation happens.  
**Why it matters:** AI credits and time are only spent on leads worth contacting. No more paying to DM spam bots or irrelevant accounts.

### AI Prompt Wizard
**What:** A 4-step guided setup that generates the AI's instructions — the "brain" behind every DM — without the user having to write a single line of prompt engineering.  
**Why it matters:** A bad prompt produces bad DMs. The wizard captures the key ingredients (niche, audience, value prop, tone, winning example) that make AI output actually sound like the user's brand, not a generic robot.

### Per-Profile AI DM Generation
**What:** For every lead, the AI reads that specific profile and writes a unique message. Not a template with a [NAME] field swapped in — a genuinely contextual opener referencing their specific bio or niche.  
**Why it matters:** This is the single biggest reason reply rates improve. Prospects can't tell it's automated. The message feels hand-written because the context is real.

### Human Approval Queue
**What:** Every AI-generated DM sits in a review queue. Nothing sends until the user explicitly approves it.  
**Why it matters:** This is what separates MagnetEngine from "bots." The user retains full control over brand voice and message quality. It's also the key reason accounts are not at risk — you're the one approving and the extension sends as you, not as an automated script bypassing Instagram's UI.

### Chrome Extension Auto-Send
**What:** Sends approved DMs through the user's actual Chrome browser with randomised human-like delays.  
**Why it matters:** Because Instagram can detect and ban API-based or headless browser automation. Sending through a real Chrome session with natural timing mimics real human behaviour and protects the account.

### Dashboard with 8 Funnel Metrics
**What:** Live tracking of every stage from lead scraped → DM sent → replied → positive reply → booking.  
**Why it matters:** Most cold outreach tools show you "messages sent" and nothing else. Knowing your reply rate, positive reply rate, and booking rate lets you diagnose exactly where your funnel is leaking and fix it.

### AI Analyst
**What:** A rule-based insights engine that reads your metrics, compares them to benchmarks, and gives specific, prioritised recommendations.  
**Why it matters:** Replaces the need for a consultant. Instead of staring at numbers and guessing why things aren't working, the user gets a plain-English diagnosis: "Your booking rate is low — add a Calendly link directly in follow-ups."

### CRM + Export
**What:** Built-in lead status management (cold/warm/won), full profile data stored, and one-click export to CSV or JSON.  
**Why it matters:** Keeps everything in one place. No copy-pasting between tools. Agencies can export clean data for clients, ad retargeting, or follow-up sequences without extra steps.

## 6. What Makes It Different

### vs. Doing it manually
Manual prospecting requires an SDR (or the founder) to: find accounts, vet each one, open their profile, write a personalised message, send it, track it in a spreadsheet. At 4–5 minutes per lead, 100 leads is an 8-hour day. MagnetEngine collapses the same 100 leads to roughly 5 minutes of search time + 15 minutes of DM review. **The work is 95% automated; the human's 5% is the approval step that keeps quality high.**

### vs. Spray-and-pray bots
Other tools blast identical templated messages at thousands of accounts at once. Instagram detects this pattern, limits reach, and sometimes bans accounts. Worse, the messages read like spam so reply rates are negligible (1–2%). MagnetEngine uses natural sending via Chrome, human-like delays, and per-profile personalisation. Users report reply rates of 15–19%, five to ten times better than generic automation.

### vs. Buying a course or hiring a VA
A $2,000 lead gen course teaches strategy but does none of the work. A VA is expensive, slow (one profile at a time), and inconsistent. MagnetEngine is the software that does the work — for a fraction of either cost — and runs 24/7.

### vs. Other AI outreach tools
Most AI outreach tools target LinkedIn or email and work best in B2B enterprise contexts. MagnetEngine is built specifically for Instagram DMs — the channel where coaches, consultants, and service businesses actually close deals. The Apify-powered scraper, the Instagram-specific profile mapping (bio, business category, verified status, city), and the Chrome extension sending are all built for this one channel and optimised for it.

### The key unfair advantage: human-in-the-loop + automation combined
MagnetEngine sits exactly at the intersection of "fast enough to scale" and "good enough to convert." Fully automated tools sacrifice quality for volume. Fully manual work sacrifices scale for quality. MagnetEngine automates the research, writing, and delivery — and gives the human exactly the one moment where their judgment matters: reading the DM and approving it.

## 9. Suggested Tone of Voice

### In one line
**Confident, direct, and results-obsessed.** We say what the tool does, show proof it works, and don't waste the reader's time.

### Characteristics

**Direct, not fluffy.** Lead with the outcome. "Your calendar fills automatically" before any explanation of how. Cut adjectives that don't add meaning.

**Contrarian toward the old way.** Use the contrast between manual/generic and intelligent/personalised as a rhetorical engine. "Most tools spray the same message at 1,000 people. MagnetEngine writes a different message for each one." This positions MagnetEngine as the smart choice without attacking competitors by name.

**ROI-anchored.** Ground every claim in a number. Not "more booked calls" — "+450% booked calls in 6 weeks." Not "saves time" — "60% of SDR time reclaimed." Sophisticated buyers (agency owners) respond to business math, not emotion.
 
**Respect the reader's intelligence.** Avoid over-explaining. These are people who run businesses; they understand the problem acutely. Acknowledge the pain briefly, then get to the solution.

**Urgency that's earned, not manufactured.** Scarcity claims (founding 20, 3 agency spots) are real product decisions, not fake countdown timers. Lean into real urgency — the cost of continuing to do outreach the old way — rather than artificial.

**Human and brand-safe.** Despite being an automation tool, MagnetEngine is explicitly about *preserving* the human voice and *protecting* accounts. Messaging should reflect that philosophy: this is a tool that makes humans better at outreach, not one that replaces them.

### Phrases that work
- "Clone yourself with AI"
- "Stop prospecting manually"
- "Your calendar on autopilot"
- "The right message, to the right person, at the right time"
- "Automation, not education"
- "Review before it sends — always"
- "One deal pays for itself"

### Phrases to avoid
- "Revolutionary" / "game-changing" (overused, no meaning)
- "Leverage synergies" or similar corporate jargon
- "Blast messages" (implies spam, wrong association)
- Any framing that suggests the account is being "taken over" or is at risk
- Overpromising specific numbers without qualification (reply rates vary by niche and offer)
