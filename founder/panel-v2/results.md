## What would flip a no

- Real results from people like me, such as a few named coaches or agency owners showing booked calls from this exact tool, plus the preview showing 10 DMs that don't read like a template. Even then I'd test it on a second account first.
- Seeing a real founding member's booked calls from this, or my guru recommending it with an affiliate link and a cheaper price like $67 a month.
- Real numbers from a few founding members in my kind of agency (DMs sent, replies, booked calls), or a way to run it without keeping a tab open on my own account.
- A price under about $70 a month, or published results from real founding members showing booked calls at my volume.
- Real results from agency owners at my size, with replies and booked calls, plus proof the preview DMs get replies from people like my prospects. A price under $60 would also let me try it without worrying.
- A few real founding members showing their reply and booked-call numbers over a couple of months with no restrictions, and a lower price or a pause-anytime option for the months I'm not doing cold outreach.
- Real results from a few founding members who are in my situation (small, under $5k a month), and a price closer to $50-ish, or a way to test it on a throwaway second account with no risk to my main one.
- Real booked-call numbers from a few named founding members who sell what I sell, or a short screen recording of someone's actual sends and replies from one account over a few weeks.
- A price near $50 to $60, or real results from founding members who did this from a second account without being restricted.
- Real results from founding members (booked calls from the DMs, not just sent counts), plus a price closer to $79 or a lower tier with fewer DMs.
- The guru I follow recommending it, or seeing real results from a founding member who sells a high-ticket program like mine, with reply and booked-call numbers.
- Two or three founding members in my niche showing real reply and booked-call numbers from a few weeks of sending, plus a clear statement of what pace counts as the safe default.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
