# MagnetEngine: one page

**Verdict (computed): Not yet.** Each client-month earns money and year 1 is profitable on the planned ramp, but 0 of 100 simulated buyers bought at today's offer (the bar is 25%).

**The problem.** Agency owners and coaches who sell $1,000+/month services win clients in Instagram DMs. Writing a real first message takes 4–5 minutes a prospect, so outreach stops whenever client work gets busy. The bots that automate it send one template to everyone and get ignored.

**The customer.** First: growing SMMA owners ($10–50k/month) who still type cold DMs by hand.

**The offer and price.** AI writes each prospect their own first DM from their profile; the owner approves every one; it sends from their own Instagram, in their own browser. **$147/month** ($1,470/year). The Founding 25 (until 2026-12-31): 7-day trial, a 10-DM Preview before any card, the Booked-Call Guarantee (300 approved DMs and no booked call → the month is refunded), and a refund if Instagram restricts the account at the default pace.

**The evidence.**
- *Panel (simulated):* 0 of 100 at today's offer. 79 of the passes were trust, not price; 29 of 30 SMMA owners accept $147. With the redesigned offer, 4 of 20 (all four growing SMMA owners).
- *Competitor gap:* single-account tools cost $9–$97/month and sell volume; none combines per-profile writing with human approval. The category has almost no independent reviews, so the first tool with real published results owns trust.
- *Real test:* not yet run. The soft open (10-26 → 11-08) needs ≥ 4 paid founders, and ≥ 2 of them with a booked call by day 30.

**What the money is for.** $2,605 covers a legal review of the guarantee and terms, the Web Store listing, ten free pilot months and small launch tests. The product is already built.

**The ask.** Twenty-five agency owners willing to run it for 30 days at the founding price and let their real numbers be published.
