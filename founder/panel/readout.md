# Panel readout (moderator's notes on results.md)

**0 buy · 100 pass.** Seed 7, 100 simulated buyers, every answer valid. Pitch: `founder/pitch-v1.md`.

## What the zero means

- **It is not a price verdict.** 67 of 100 buyers gave price answers where $147 is *not* "too expensive". By segment, the share of buyers for whom $147 sits under their own "too expensive" line:
  - Growing SMMA owners ($10–50k/mo): **29 of 30**. Medians: a bargain at $79, getting expensive at $197, too expensive at $350.
  - Coaches: **23 of 25**. Medians: a bargain at $59, getting expensive at $150, too expensive at $250.
  - SDRs: 4 of 10. Medians: a bargain at $49, getting expensive at $94, too expensive at $139.
  - Early-stage owners under $5k/mo: **11 of 35**. Medians: a bargain at $39, getting expensive at $80, too expensive at $130.
- **It is a risk-reversal verdict.** Reason codes: trust 79, price 9, need 8, timing 3, quality 1. In the flip answers, **100 of 100 ask for proof** (named results, booked calls). **81 of 100 ask for a longer trial, a refund window or no card.** 32 ask for a lower price, and almost all of those are early-stage. 25 want an answer about account risk or a way to run it on a secondary account. 13 want to see sample DMs for their own niche before paying. 10 want a guru's endorsement.
- The three terms named most often together: **no results + card required + "payments are final" after 3 days.** Buyers read that combination as "relying on people forgetting to cancel" (P042) and "a trap" (P018, P031, P093). Several also say three days is too short to see a single reply (P004, P009, P026, P053).

## Who is closest to yes

Growing SMMA owners and high-ticket coaches. They accept the price and feel the pain; trust is their one blocker. Early-stage owners under $5k/mo are a price problem *and* a trust problem. Do not lead with them.

## Honest limits

- These are simulated buyers. Language models lean agreeable, so a 0% here is a strong negative, but it is still not a forecast. Read buy rates as an upper bound.
- **The pitch was harsher than the live page in one way.** It said "No customer results published yet" out loud; the site simply has none. Real visitors notice an absence less than a sentence, so the live page may convert somewhat better than this panel. It cannot convert well with no proof. The panel's ask for proof was unanimous, and every buyer reached it on their own.
- One buyer (P086) mentioned "the $197 figure in the project docs". That agent read beyond its brief. Its answer stays in, but treat it with that in mind.
- Panel quotes are research. Never use them as testimonials.
